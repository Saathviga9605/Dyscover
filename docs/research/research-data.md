# Research data

Legacy pages recorded fixation duration, fixation count, target/distractor dwell, TTFF, switches, reaction time, hesitation, saccade variance, regressions, scanpath entropy, and horizontal bias. Stage 1 names these fields without claiming validation.

Future collection should be consent-based, minimized, access-controlled, and exportable by an admin/research workflow. CSV is an export format, not the primary store. WebGazer should feed a collector, calibration layer, fixation engine, AOI mapper, and feature extractor rather than individual games.
